Turn the cultural early-support screenshot into a verifiable record with a stable URL, without pretending a local card is proof.

Milestone: **M2** · Priority: **P0** · Effort: **M** · Workstream: **identity**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create receipts from committed server backing events, with unique IDs and no client-set timestamps or listener values.
- [ ] Show artist identity, committed/executed/metric-observed times, source snapshot, committed Credits and later exit/correction context.
- [ ] Provide a verification route and shareable card; unchanged public URL verifies against server history after refresh/device change.
- [ ] Keep receipt evidence after selling, respect privacy/deletion policy and state what the record actually proves.
