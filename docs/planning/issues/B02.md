- [ ] Show monthly-listener values, 7/30/90-day range controls and range growth in the editorial app layout.

- [ ] Preserve actual verified observations and outages; support empty, one-point and invalid-reading states without backfill.
- [ ] Verify the chart and 320px layout in isolated browser tests without changing backing or recording behavior.
