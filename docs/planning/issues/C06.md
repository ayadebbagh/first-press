Milestone: **M2** · Priority: **P0** · Effort: **M** · Workstream: **data**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Use agreed UTC schedule and unique batch/job IDs; restarting or running two workers cannot settle the same order twice.
- [ ] Allow bounded retries/backoff and manual replay; record job results and lag.
- [ ] Collect/validate first, then settle only eligible valid snapshots; source failure does not fabricate a new price.
- [ ] Test overlapping workers, missed interval, crash after commit and recovery without duplicate ledger or receipt events.
