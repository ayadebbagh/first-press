Keep the curated beta manageable and turn local-only suggestions into a real review queue.

Milestone: **M2** · Priority: **P1** · Effort: **M** · Workstream: **data**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Members can submit artist/profile suggestions with duplicate suppression and rate limits.
- [ ] Only admins can admit, edit, suspend or reject artists, review identities and manage playback provenance.
- [ ] New entries remain unbackable until reviewed identity and baseline meet policy; provide a user-facing status.
- [ ] Record who changed catalog/eligibility and why; test ordinary members cannot invoke admin actions.
