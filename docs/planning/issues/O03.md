Keep collection and settlement failures visible without buying an observability stack prematurely.

Milestone: **M2** · Priority: **P0** · Effort: **M** · Workstream: **operations**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Provide structured redacted logs and counters for snapshot age, collection success, queue age, failed settlements and ledger reconciliation.
- [ ] Separate liveness/readiness and show an accurate user-facing last-update/stale state.
- [ ] Define alert thresholds, operator ownership and runbooks for worker outage, bad data and unavailable DB.
- [ ] Rehearse an outage and ensure retries/recovery are visible without leaking identities or tokens.
