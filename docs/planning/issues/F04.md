Turn the accepted 1,000-Credit, artist-only, twice-daily queued backing concept into a versioned contract before implementing live balances.

Milestone: **M0** · Priority: **P0** · Effort: **M** · Workstream: **foundation**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Specify integer centi-Credits, position-unit precision, rounding direction, minimum order and any stake limits.
- [ ] Choose UTC update boundaries, submission cutoff, eligible snapshot time, delayed observations, stale grace, cancellation cutoff and suspended-artist behavior.
- [ ] Provide worked examples for buy, loss, partial/full sale, cancelled order, delayed update and source correction.
- [ ] Separate committed-at, executed-at and metric-observed-at timestamps; decide which proves early support.
- [ ] Leave weekly top-ups disabled and unspecified until D06; no cash/redemption or cross-platform substitution.

## Dependencies

No implementation dependency. Resolve the decision or spike on its own merits.
